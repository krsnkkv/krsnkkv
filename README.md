### 🧠 How do we understand another mind?

We constantly make inferences about what other people know, believe, intend, and feel. How do we do it? And could we build machines that do something similar?

I'm interested in **Theory of Mind** — the ability to reason about other minds — and what it can teach us about human cognition, intelligence, and the way we build AI.

I want to understand not just *what* an intelligent system predicts, but how it represents beliefs, forms expectations, and reasons about perspectives beyond its own.

### 🔬 What I'm exploring

- **Theory of Mind:** How do we attribute beliefs, intentions, and knowledge to others?
- **Cognitive modelling:** Can we build computational models that help explain how humans think and perceive?
- **Interpretable AI:** Can we understand the internal reasoning of artificial systems, rather than treating them as black boxes?
- **Neuroscience & intelligence:** What can neural mechanisms tell us about learning, representation, and cognition?
